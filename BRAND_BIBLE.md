# BRAND_BIBLE.md: The ESGAY Manifesto & Style Guide

## I. The Mission: End-Stage Gay Agenda Yardsale (ESGAY)
The old systems are decaying; we are signaling the collapse of the establishment. ESGAY is an "Edgy Luxe" sanctuary for the radicalized, the subversive, and the unapologetic. We are a semi-curated marketplace rejecting mass-produced "slop" in favor of high-quality craftsmanship, biting wit, and subversive luxury.

## II. The Five Creative North Stars
Every asset, line of code, and product must be filtered through these specific identities:

*   **Christian Lacroix:** High-fashion maximalism and heavy textures.
*   **John Waters' Divine:** Transgressive "filth," camp-as-weaponry, and unapologetic satire.
*   **Tricky (Massive Attack):** Brooding, industrial grit and low-fi underground atmosphere.
*   **Siouxsie Sioux:** Sharp Goth-punk tailoring and aggressive, cold elegance.
*   **Robert Polidori (Havana):** The backdrop of "Beautiful Decay"—faded grandiosity and historical collapse.

## II. Terminology
*   **The Courtyards:** The primary navigational sectors of the Embassy.
*   **The Artifacts:** The curated products available for acquisition.
*   **The Manifest:** A record of a user's acquired artifacts.
*   **Moving Image:** Sophisticated term for film or video works.

## III. Brand Architecture: The Abandoned Embassy
ESGAY is not a storefront; it is an Abandoned Embassy representing sovereign territory within the decaying ruins of Western capitalism.

### The Rooms (Navigation)
*   **/the-galleries:** Fine art and digital subversion exhibitions.
*   **/the-yardsmiths:** Heavy, tangible crafts (forged glass, jewelry, furniture).
*   **/sartorial-spite:** High-end, tailored fashion designed to intimidate.
*   **/propaganda-parody:** Biting satire and cultural contraband.
*   **/the-apothecary:** Luxe beauty, skincare, and bold pigments.

## IV. Visual Identity & UI
**The Glitch Layer:** The UI features "glitched" CSS layers that appear to peel away like old plaster to reveal radical underground messages.

### The Palette of Decay:
*   **Primary Base:** Obsidian Matte (Heavy shadows).
*   **Radical Accent:** Anarchist Crimson (Dried-blood red).
*   **Luxe Contrast:** Tarnished Gold (Satirical nod to corporate wealth).
*   **Havana Mid-tones:** Peeling Turquoise and Faded Ochre.

### Typography:
*   **The Luxe (Headings):** High-contrast Serif (e.g., Editorial New).
*   **The Punk (Body/Meta):** Monospaced (e.g., Space Mono).

## V. Verbal Identity: Poetic Subversion
**Tone:** A mix of high-fashion sophistication and biting satirical wit. Use rhythmic, poetic language to deliver sharp messages.

**The Anti-PC Boundary:** We embrace the transgressive "filth" of Divine; we do not apologize for being "too much".

**Forbidden Jargon:** Strictly ban hollow corporate terms like "synergy," "customer-centric," or "optimization".

## VI. Operational Standards
**Visual Merchandising:** All items must follow the principle of "Inverses"—pairing decayed beauty with beautiful decay (e.g., a luxe necklace on crumbling molding).

**The Unboxing Ritual:** Documentation must resemble weathered customs forms or confiscated documents. Packaging must utilize upcycled luxury materials.
